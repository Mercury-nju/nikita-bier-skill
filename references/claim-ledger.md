# Claim Ledger

This ledger prevents a memorable post from becoming a universal law. A claim is usable only with its precondition and a way it could be wrong.

| ID | Reconstructed claim | Evidence | Tier | Precondition | What would weaken it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q01 | A product must produce a meaningful human outcome before distribution can be interpreted. | S01, S02, S03 | A | The product has a distinct first-value event. | Users repeatedly acquire and retain without that event. |
| Q02 | Faster time to value is useful when it does not remove the state that creates relevance. | S01, S02 | A | Setup is not itself the value. | Longer setup materially increases activated use or trust. |
| Q03 | Manual help can reveal maximum appeal but does not prove self-serve demand. | S03 | A | Staff can supply part of the experience. | Users reproduce the result after help is removed. |
| Q04 | Network density can be a prerequisite and a cost of the product. | S04, S12, S16 | A/C | Value depends on relevant peers in the same window. | Individual value remains strong with sparse peers. |
| Q05 | Repeated community tests require comparable, independent communities. | S04, S11 | A/B | Multiple communities exist and exposure can be contained. | Communities are porous or only one central graph matters. |
| Q06 | A concentrated launch is a constraint response when the graph must be saturated at once. | S04, S12 | A/C | One community is central and partial exposure is misleading. | A bounded independent test can create the same value state. |
| Q07 | A credible critical path protects the validity of a demand test. | S03, S14, S15 | A | Users can reject the product before considering its value. | A well-executed path still fails with the target user. |
| Q08 | The interesting property of a product may be more valuable than a broad feature set. | C05 | A | The property creates a recognizable value event. | Focused use does not lead to repeat value or qualified acquisition. |
| Q09 | Distribution advantages are temporary and platform-dependent. | S07, S08 | A/B | The mechanism depends on a platform surface or permission. | The mechanism remains effective across changed constraints. |
| Q10 | Sharing is evidence of distribution only when it produces qualified first value. | S05, S08 | B | Shared output can reach a suitable user. | Shares reliably lead to activated users through another path. |
| Q11 | Virality and durability require separate measurements and explanations. | S01, S06 | A | The product can be acquired faster than it is retained. | The product's intended use is one-time or naturally seasonal. |
| Q12 | Replacing a main inbox imposes a reliability and reach burden that a narrow messaging feature may not. | S09 | A | Users would move existing conversations. | The feature completes a task without competing with the main inbox. |
| Q13 | Interest-graph activation depends on relevant initial content, not contact import alone. | S10 | B | The product's value is a personalized feed. | Contacts themselves are the valuable content. |
| Q14 | Researching strong existing flows is a product-development input. | S01, C07 | A/B | The product competes on a familiar interaction or permission. | The comparison product serves a different job or constraint. |
| Q15 | Product integrity includes anticipating spam, manipulation, and unintended use. | S12, S17 | C | The system has incentives that can be gamed. | Misuse is implausible or isolated without user impact. |
| Q16 | Temporary products can create real value without becoming durable networks. | S06 | A | The product's promise is compatible with temporary use. | The business depends on long-term retention or habit. |
| Q17 | A platform metric can improve while the underlying human signal worsens. | S17 | B | Ranking or incentives shape what users see. | The metric is directly tied to user-perceived value and remains robust to gaming. |
| Q18 | A product's test geometry determines how much iteration is possible. | S04 | A | Community overlap and density affect exposure. | The product can be tested without network state. |

## Use rule

When a response relies on a claim, retrieve its case and state the precondition. If no case supplies a precondition or falsifier, present the statement as an open hypothesis rather than as “Nikita's principle.”
