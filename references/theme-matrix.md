# Theme Matrix

Use this matrix when you want to connect a principle back to the public materials that support it.

## Dense Networks And Teens

Supported by:

- Lenny interview/transcript
- tbh and Gas reporting
- X corpus posts about adults, schools, and closed networks

Working conclusion:

- start in dense, repeated-contact communities
- assume adult acquisition is far less viral

## Dead-On-Arrival Orchestration

Supported by:

- January 2022 founder thread
- repeated X posts about network activation and validation
- X corpus posts about closed communities versus mega launches

Working conclusion:

- many ideas fail because the value state cannot be repeatedly orchestrated

## Replace Launch With Test

Supported by:

- June 2024 thread
- May 2025 X post on mega launches versus repeated experiments
- Lenny interview comments on fast iteration

Working conclusion:

- consumer products do not get many clean retries
- treat launch design as exposure control

## Do Things That Don't Scale

Supported by:

- August 2024 thread
