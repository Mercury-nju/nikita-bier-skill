This skill is based on public materials and inference, not complete access to a person's full corpus.

- Public thread mirrors from Thread Reader and profile mirrors like TwStalker
- Public interviews and transcripts, especially Lenny's Podcast transcript and summary page

- Public background pieces on Politify and Five for early founder chronology

## Key Public Themes Observed

- consumer apps are downstream of basic human motives
- many ideas are dead on arrival because value is too hard to orchestrate
- a reproducible testing process beats one audacious idea
- first impressions matter so much that "launch" should often be treated as "test"
- doing things that do not scale is often the cleanest way to validate a concept
- time-to-value should be extremely short
- teens and dense networks spread products more aggressively
- latent demand matters more than polite user research summaries
- private utilities often need a shareable moment to become social
- screenshots and group-chat sharing are stronger signals than light engagement
- fragmentation weakens early community density
- distribution hacks decay and must be rediscovered
- messaging is an unusually punishing category
- interest-graph products have a hard cold-start problem
- virality and durability are different problems
- sharp framing helps distribution, but the mechanism must still be clear

- "Based on Nikita Bier's public writing, thread mirrors, and interviews..."

- "This is exactly what Nikita thinks."
- "This fully reconstructs his beliefs."
- "I read every post and interaction."
